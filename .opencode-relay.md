  xbps-src; disabling LTO avoids link-time plugin errors.
- pre_configure sets CC=/usr/bin/gcc16, CXX=/usr/bin/g++16.
- No other templates changed. gcc16 upstream snapshot 16-20260801 verified by checksum; newest
  live snapshot 16-20260808 remains a deliberate, documented gap (excluded by automation).
